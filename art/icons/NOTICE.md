# UI icons

`ui_icon_gen.py` takes the shapes of the UI icons (everything in `ui_icons.json`) from
**Noto Color Emoji** by Google, licensed under the Apache License 2.0
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji). They are reduced to 64×64 pixel grids with
a small palette and a 2-px dark outline. The fish and bird icons (`icon_gen.py`) are drawn
from scratch.
